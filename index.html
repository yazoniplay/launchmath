03 / FAQ

Before you launch.

The essentials, without the fine-print maze.

What is Launch Math?
Launch Math is designed to help digital creators evaluate product pricing, estimate per-sale proceeds, account for selling costs, and plan launches using adjustable financial assumptions.
Is the $29 payment recurring?
No. The intended offer is a one-time $29 payment for lifetime access. Your checkout provider must be configured to sell a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
Which selling platforms are supported?
The planned fee-analysis workflow is designed around configurable platform fees and transaction costs. Confirm the actual supported platforms and their available fee presets in your product before advertising specific integrations.
Are the forecasts guaranteed?
No. Forecasts depend on the assumptions entered, including sales volume, conversion rates, pricing, fees, and expenses. Actual results can differ. Launch Math is a planning tool, not a guarantee of revenue or profit.
Does Launch Math require installation?
Launch Math is intended to run in a modern web browser. The exact availability of saved projects, offline access, account features, and device synchronization depends on the implemented product.
